## Evidence-driven plan adjustments

Update the plan as implementation reveals a necessary adjustment. Record the
observation/test failure, original assumption, proposed correction, why it is
needed, affected requirement IDs, impact on acceptance and verification. Record
consequential decisions in GRADUATION_DECISIONS.md. Routine implementation choices
need no approval. Do not adjust the plan merely to make a failing check disappear,
increase a budget to obtain a pass, omit a requirement, move its gate past a later
stage, remove uncertainty, or substitute an easier behavior.

If an adjustment genuinely conflicts with the approved research scope or requires
new authorization, ask a focused question and continue independent authorized
work. Document unresolved questions rather than inventing approval.
